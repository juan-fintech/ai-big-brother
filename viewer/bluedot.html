THANK YOU, BLUEDOT

Thanks to BlueDot Impact, where Greg and Juan met. BlueDot Impact are a fantastic organisation that have made it their mission to build the workforce that protects humanity.

AI is the most transformative technology humanity has ever produced. Too few people are working to make it go well. BlueDot find people who could help, and they support them to go full-time working on solving important problems. They also provide the most amazing free courses on AI safety and governance! Check them out!
